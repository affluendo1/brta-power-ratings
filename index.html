V3 METHODOLOGY

A smaller, cleaner model

V2 separately rewarded the game score and the match win. V3 removes that double-counting. A score such as 6–1 already contains the fact that the player won, so the model fits one coherent game-score likelihood and derives match strength from the same latent ability.

1 · Latent strength

Each player has strength θᵢ. Regularization anchors the section, and the fitted mean is centred on zero.

Σ θᵢ = 0
2 · Game probability

Opponent strength enters through the difference between the two latent ratings.

p(i,j)=1/[1+exp(−(θᵢ−θⱼ)/0.75)]
3 · Score likelihood

Every game won and lost contributes. A 6–1 therefore carries stronger evidence than a 7–6 without needing a second W/L bonus.

ℒₘ = gᵢ ln(p) + gⱼ ln(1−p)
4 · Real-time recency

Decay uses actual elapsed days, not round numbers. Development validation selected a 365-day half-life.

wₘ = 2^(−Δdays/365)
5 · Empirical-Bayes shrinkage

Sparse players are pulled harder toward the section mean. The penalty strength λ=5 was selected by walk-forward validation rather than hand-picked.

penalty = (5/2) Σ θᵢ²
6 · Display scale

The public number remains easy to read and keeps 1000 as section average.

Powerᵢ = 1000 + 250θᵢ
7 · Uncertainty

The inverse curvature of the fitted posterior gives an approximate variance for each player. More and better-connected evidence narrows it.

Cov(θ) ≈ H⁻¹
SE(Powerᵢ)=250√(H⁻¹ᵢᵢ)
8 · Opponent reliability

V3 does not bolt on an arbitrary reliability multiplier. Low-data opponents are naturally shrunk toward 1000 and carry wider uncertainty, so shaky evidence has less leverage.

θᵢ ~ N(0, 1/λ)

Complete objective

maximize J(θ)=Σₘ wₘ[gᵢ ln pₘ + gⱼ ln(1−pₘ)] − (5/2)Σᵢθᵢ²

Why these constants?

The half-life and shrinkage were selected with walk-forward development validation across five BRTA section datasets: train on earlier rounds, predict a later round, then move forward. On those folds V3 reduced held-out per-game negative log loss from 0.6640 to 0.6406 and match-outcome Brier score from 0.1683 to 0.1632 versus V2. This is development validation, not a claim of perfect external calibration.

Deliberately excluded: subjective excuses, string breaks, nerves, weather, “should have won” points and other context that is not recorded consistently for every player. Those belong in match analysis, not the rating equation.

Players below four completed singles matches still participate in the opponent network; they are only hidden from the published leaderboard.
