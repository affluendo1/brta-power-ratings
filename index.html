V3 METHODOLOGY

Four views, one scale

Singles

One latent strength per player.

p=logistic((θᵢ−θⱼ)/0.75)
Doubles pairings

One latent strength per recurring partnership.

p=logistic((θpair A−θpair B)/0.75)
Individual doubles

Each doubles side is the mean strength of its two players, allowing partners and opponents to be disentangled.

θteam=(θA+θB)/2
Overall

Simple and inspectable rather than mysterious.

Overall=(Singles+Doubles)/2
Display scale
Power=1500+600θ
Score likelihood
ℒ=gᵢln(p)+gⱼln(1−p)

Player Lab

The profile overlay is explanatory, not a second rating model. Form, best performances, confidence and the Rating Explorer help show why the fitted rating looks the way it does.
