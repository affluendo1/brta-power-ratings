V3 METHODOLOGY

Same engine, two networks

Singles players and doubles pairings are fitted in separate opponent networks using the same V3 scoreline model. A doubles partnership is treated as one rating entity, regardless of the order the two names were entered.

Game probability
p(i,j)=1/[1+exp(−(θᵢ−θⱼ)/0.75)]
Score likelihood
ℒₘ=gᵢ ln(p)+gⱼ ln(1−p)
Recency
wₘ=2^(−Δdays/365)
Display scale
Powerᵢ=1500+600θᵢ

Adaptive bands

Only qualified entries determine the colour boundaries. Provisional entries can therefore never distort the bands.

σ*=1.4826 · median(|Rᵢ−median(R)|)

Band boundaries: M + {1.50, 0.75, 0.20, −0.35, −1.00, −1.65}σ*. Provisional entries are always grey even if their estimated rating falls inside a coloured band.
