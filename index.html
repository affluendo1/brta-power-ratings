METHODOLOGY

How the rating works

All players are solved simultaneously. Changing one result can alter the ratings of players who never faced each other because evidence propagates through the opponent network.

1 · Latent strength

Each player has strength θᵢ, centred on the section.

Σ θᵢ = 0
2 · Game probability
pᴳ(i,j)=1/[1+exp(−(θᵢ−θⱼ)/0.75)]
3 · Match probability
pᴹ(i,j)=1/[1+exp(−(θᵢ−θⱼ)/0.55)]
4 · Recency
wᵣ = 0.90^(R−r)
5 · Regularization
penalty=(0.75/2)Σθᵢ²
6 · Display scale
Powerᵢ=1000+250θᵢ

Complete objective

maximize J(θ)=Σₘ wₘ[ℒᴳ,ₘ+1.8ℒᴹ,ₘ]−(0.75/2)Σᵢθᵢ²

Players below four matches remain in the fitted opponent network. They are only hidden from the published leaderboard.
